# Code of Conduct

noobtopro is a small, private project, but everyone who interacts with it —
contributors, reviewers, and users — deserves a respectful, harassment-free
experience. This is a brief, Contributor-Covenant-style summary of what we
expect.

## Our standard

We pledge to make participation a welcoming experience regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback (PR reviews included).
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy toward other people.

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (e.g. a physical or email address)
  without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention. The product is
  used by, and marketed toward, young learners — keep all content and
  interactions appropriate for that audience.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies within all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull
requests, and reviews) and when an individual is representing the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainer at **1crushhaus@gmail.com**. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainer will
respect the privacy and security of the reporter. Maintainers may remove, edit,
or reject contributions that do not align with this Code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate.
