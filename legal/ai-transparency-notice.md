## How noobtopro uses AI (user-facing)
**You are interacting with an AI system.** noobtopro uses an AI system to read the reasoning in
your math/physics/chemistry work and help generate feedback and a numeric **rank (0–350)**. The
underlying model is provided by **[Groq / model provider]**; the grading application, rubric
logic, and final rank are built and operated by us. **Feedback and ranks are produced with the
help of an AI system, not a human examiner.**

**What the rank is — and is NOT.** A **relative learning signal** to help you improve. It is
**NOT** an accredited exam/qualification/certificate; **NOT** an admissions, enrollment,
scholarship, employment, or eligibility decision (and is not shared by us with any school or
employer); **NOT** a guarantee of ability; **NOT** a substitute for a qualified teacher.

**How the rank is produced.** (1) You submit typed answers and/or photos. (2) The AI analyses the
**reasoning** against a rubric. (3) **Our servers** compute the final 0–350 rank from that rubric
(the model does not set the number directly). The rank is **relative** to a reference population,
so the same work may map differently over time.

**AI can be wrong.** It can misread handwriting, misinterpret reasoning, miss a correct
alternative method, or make mistakes — especially with unclear photos or unusual notation. Treat
your rank as **informational guidance, not a verdict.** Do not make important decisions based
solely on a noobtopro rank.

**Human review.** If you think a rank is wrong, you can **request human review and contest the
result** at **[link/email/in-app path]**. Tell us why you disagree and we will take it into
account; we aim to respond within **[timeframe]**.

**Your data.** See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy), including the automated-processing section.
**Who this is for:** adults **18 and over**.
